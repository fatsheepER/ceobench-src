# Shared business workspace

This guide applies only to shared-v1 teams. Other layouts use the workspace and memory paths in their system instructions.

All roles share scripts/, data/, outputs/ and these plans:
plans/commercial.md: Customer groups, channels, prices, promotions, advertising and conversion or renewal assumptions. Suggested maintainer: Growth.
plans/service.md: Model tiers, capacity, usage quotas, operations, development and service risks. Suggested maintainer: OpsFinance.
plans/cash.md: Baseline and candidate forecasts, final CEO forecast references, assumptions and due forecast reviews. Suggested maintainer: OpsFinance.
plans/decisions.md: Chosen actions, confirmed business results and references, pending work, owners and review dates. Suggested maintainer: CEO.
Keep the plans current. Anyone may edit them.
Distinguish proposed actions from confirmed business results in your saved work.
Use focused questions to coordinate work when needed.
Keep scripts in scripts/ and larger tables or outputs in data/ or outputs/.
All roles may read and write memory/ceo.md, memory/growth.md and memory/ops_finance.md.
Only your own role memory is loaded automatically each week.
